We chose the mental health inpatient activity data. Our ideas are revolving around joining two or more datasets in order to find a relationship between several variables, e.g. how do different variables (age, diagnosis, hospital) affect the length of stay?

During our first workshop we had the idea of joining the data by diagnosis, since it will exclude any patients with missing diagnosis, and also provide the patients’ characteristics.

Initial ideas:

  • Number of patients in each age range

    • Percentage of male and female in each age range

  • Length of stay and diagnosis for each age range / sex

    • Would health beliefs impact length of stay/diagnosis?

    • Would area of admission impact length of stay/diagnosis?

  • How has awareness of mental health evolved over time?

    • Frequency of stays

  • Number of admissions during each year/time period

    • What external factors would effect this?

    • Percentage of male/females. Did numbers change over time? Did men become more accepting to health care?

    • Percentage of age ranges. Did numbers change over time?
